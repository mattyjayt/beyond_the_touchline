# Technical event-data metrics (per 90) for PL wing duels and defending

Scope: event-derived variables, their definitions, rough per-90 ranges by position, and reported values for Doku, White, Rice, Timber, Mosquera and Saka, for 2023/24 to 2025/26.

Access caveat: FBref (403), Squawka (403) and several Opta pages blocked automated fetches. Most player values below come from Opta Analyst, premierleague.com, Statmuse, FootyStats and search snippets of FBref pages. Where a number comes only from a search-engine snippet of a page I could not open, it is marked **(snippet)**. Positional ranges without a citation are marked **analyst estimate**. They are there to help build the simulator. They are not sourced facts.

## Attacking and wide metrics: take-ons, carries, crosses, SCA, box touches, xG/xA (and how far Doku is an outlier)

### Takeaway
Doku is the biggest take-on outlier in Premier League data. He attempted about 10.8 dribbles/take-ons per 90 in 2024/25 (Kudus was next at 6.55, Sulemana 7.24) and completed about 6.8 per 90 at about 62%. That is roughly 1.5 to 2 times the next-best PL players and well above Yamal (9.3 attempts) or Vinícius (8.0) across the top five leagues. In 2025/26 his volume fell to about 9.5 attempts per 90 and about 4.3 successful per 90, a success rate of about 52%, but he still leads the league. League-average take-on success is about 37%.

### Inferences
- **Winger ranges (analyst estimate, Opta/FBref-style definitions; PL wingers with 900+ minutes):**
  - Take-on attempts: about 2–4 per 90 typical; about 5–7 elite; Doku 9.5–10.8 is an outlier.
  - Take-on success: about 35–50% typical, against a league average near 37% (cited); Doku about 52–62%.
  - Successful take-ons: about 1–2 typical; about 3–4 elite; Doku 4.3–6.8.
  - Progressive carries (FBref definition): about 3–6 per 90.
  - Carries into the final third: about 1.5–3 per 90. Carries into the penalty area: about 1–2.5 per 90.
  - Crosses: about 2–5 per 90 (cross accuracy about 20–30%).
  - SCA: about 3–5 per 90 (elite about 5+).
  - Touches in the opposition box: about 4–8 per 90 (elite inverted wingers about 7–10).
  - npxG: about 0.2–0.4 per 90. xA/xAG: about 0.15–0.35 per 90.
- Doku's per-90 figures from different providers do not match. Volume metrics, progressive carry counts in particular, change with the definition (Opta "progressive carry" vs FBref's 10-yard rule). Measured against the league average, Doku's take-on volume is roughly 3 standard deviations above the mean, which makes him a good "extreme dribbler" archetype for the simulator.
- His volume is much higher as a substitute (17.6 vs 7.9 per 90). Tired opposing fullbacks plus small-sample effects are a plausible reason. That supports a fatigue term in the matchup model.

- I found no published positional distributions (medians/percentiles) for wingers. The ranges above are estimates.

## Defending metrics: tackles, dribblers tackled %, challenges lost, interceptions, blocks, clearances, recoveries, aerials, fouls, pressures (fullback and CB)

### Takeaway
Public per-90 values for the named Arsenal defenders are thin and partly snippet-level. Mosquera (2025/26, about 994 minutes): about 2.9 tackles, 0.7 interceptions and 1.8 aerials won per 90. White (2025/26, small sample): about 1.9 tackles, 1.3 interceptions and 0.9 dribbled past per 90. Timber 2024/25 is known only as season totals (36 tackles won, 24 interceptions in 26.9 90s, about 1.3 and 0.9 per 90). For reference, a top-percentile CB clears about 7.6 per 90 and a top-band CB wins about 3 aerials per 90.

## Definition differences: Opta vs FBref vs StatsBomb

### Takeaway
"Take-on", "dribble" and "contest" are the same concept under different names. Opta defines a take-on as "a player attempting to move around a defender while in possession of the ball". The defender's side of a successful take-on is "challenge lost" (Opta and FBref since 2022), or "dribbled past" (StatsBomb and old FBref). Progressive carry and pass thresholds differ between providers, so absolute numbers are only comparable within one provider.

### Cited Findings
- Opta take-on: "a player attempting to move around a defender while in possession of the ball" — [Opta Analyst](https://theanalyst.com/articles/premier-league-dribblers-stats-2025-26)
- The premierleague.com article uses "dribble" for a take-on and gives no formal definition — [Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4293725)
- FIFA's definition: a take-on is a player with the ball engaging opponents to get past them. It is incomplete if the player loses the ball, or if the opponent can still apply pressure from the side or front — [FIFA Training Centre](https://www.fifatrainingcentre.com/en/resources-tools/football-language/in-possession/distributing-the-ball/ball-progression/take-on/index.php)
- FotMob labels the same stat "successful dribbles" (internal key `won_contest`). Opta calls take-ons "contests" — [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/leagues/47/stats/season/23685/players/won_contest/premier-league)
- Provider snapshots disagree on Doku's 2024/25 successful take-ons per 90: 6.77 from the PL vs 6.4 from FotMob — [Premier League](https://www.premierleague.com/en/news/4293725); [FotMob](https://www.fotmob.com/leagues/47/stats/season/23685/players/won_contest/premier-league)

### Inferences
These come from prior knowledge and could not be verified against a glossary in this session.

- In October 2022, FBref switched its advanced-data supplier from StatsBomb to Opta. Under Opta:
  - "Pressures" stopped being published.
  - "Dribbles" became "Take-Ons" (Att, Succ, Succ%, Tkld, Tkld%).
  - "Dribbled past" became "Challenges lost" (Tkl% = dribblers tackled / (dribblers tackled + challenges lost)).
  - "Dribblers tackled" is shown under the Challenges header.
- FBref's progressive carry is a carry that moves the ball at least 10 yards towards the opponent's goal (measured over the last six passes or since the carry began), or any carry into the penalty area. It excludes carries starting in the defending 40% of the pitch. Its progressive pass rule is similar, also excluding the defending 40%.
- Opta and Analyst "progressive carry" figures use a different rule: roughly at least 5 m upfield, scaled by pitch zone. So Doku's "17.2 progressive carries per 90" (Opta, 2023) and "9.76" (2025) are not on the FBref scale.
- In Opta a tackle is a ground challenge where the defender dispossesses the player. Tackles won means the tackler's team gains possession.
- Opta counts take-ons, tackles and challenges as ground duels. Aerial duels are counted separately. Duels won therefore inflates for high-take-on wingers (Doku leading the PL in duels won is consistent with this).
- StatsBomb "pressure" is applying pressure on the ball carrier within about 5 yards. There is no Opta equivalent on FBref after 2022, so pressing should be modelled with recoveries, tackles in the attacking third, or PPDA instead.
- **For the simulator schema:** use one take-on event with an outcome, credited to both the attacker and the nearest defender (as a challenge won or lost). Derive tackles won % and dribblers tackled % from the same events so arithmetic stays consistent.
